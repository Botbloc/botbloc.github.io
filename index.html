At BotBloc, we are on a mission to democratize robotics by empowering individuals and businesses to create their own customizable robots. We believe that robotics should be accessible to everyone, from small-scale businesses to hobbyists, and we aim to foster a thriving community of developers and users to innovate together.

Our robotics solution combines hardware and software, and serves as a foundation for building diverse, multi-purpose robots. Starting with a simple, affordable core, you can add custom hardware and software to tailor the robot for various industries, from manufacturing to personal home use.

Universal Robot Core

Our Universal Robot Core is the heart of every BotBloc creation. With a powerful computing module and flexible mounting points, the core allows users to add custom hardware like arms, wheels, and cameras. Whether you're building a robot for manufacturing or household chores, this core scales with your needs, offering endless possibilities at an affordable price.

Online Marketplace

BotBloc’s Online Marketplace is where innovation meets collaboration. Here, you can purchase hardware components, download specialized software, and even share or trade custom robot configurations. The marketplace accelerates development, enabling users to access pre-built solutions or offer their own modules for others.

Customized Solutions

At BotBloc, we understand that one size does not fit all. Whether you're automating a warehouse, developing a personal assistant, or designing an educational tool, our flexible design enables deep customization. Our team can help you look for components and software that fit your requirements for specialized tasks and customize in the way you like.
